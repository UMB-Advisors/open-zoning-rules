## Mutation test

### How the stage changed

The Z0.3 stage flipped 10 random symbols among every table-row token. A random flip almost never lands on a row that a passing cell cites, so the stage was changed in this step:

- **Fix 1: targeted flips.** The stage flips every symbol on up to 10 rows that hold the row label of a passing baseline table cell with a non-blank symbol. It reports a noise floor: the status-change rate of every other cell.
- **Fix 5: citations left alone.** A symbol next to "." is not a token, so citations such as "Sec.3.01.02.C.24" are not rewritten.
- **Fix 6: the table's own symbols, outcomes classed.**
  - Flips use the table's most frequent conditional symbol. The stage no longer writes SE into a table whose key has only P and C.
  - Flipped cells are classed by outcome, and only a move in the expected direction counts toward `expected_rate`. A dropped cell is counted separately.
- **Fix 7: both text layers.** On the table text, a use-table segment page is the raw page plus a `[reconstructed table text]` block. The stage picks eligible lines from both blocks of the segment. But its mirror into `cache/text/<source>.txt` replaced the whole page block, and that combined string does not occur in the full text, where the table text sits after `TABLE_TEXT_MARKER`. So the replace silently did nothing.
  - The raw part is now replaced in the raw layer and the table part in the table layer.
  - A changed part that cannot be found now raises an error.
  - This was found and fixed before either table-text mutation run.

The runs use the extractor (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, `prompts/extract.v1.md`) with text only, seed 0 and n = 10. The stage counts the raw-layer line and the table-layer line of a row as two rows against n.

**What the extractor saw.** The stage samples eligible lines independently. It does not flip a row's raw copy and its table copy together. So in both runs the mutated segment mostly contradicted itself: the table text showed the flipped symbol and the raw text still showed the original.
- **Douglas:** all 8 flipped lines are table-text rows. The raw text has no line holding both a label and its symbols, so it kept every original symbol.
- **Loudoun:** 9 of the 10 flipped lines are table-text rows. Only the Data Center row (line 3044) was flipped in the raw text as well.

### Criterion, set before the runs

Commit 8162cda, made before either run, set the criterion:
- at least 2 flipped cells;
- `expected_rate` of at least 0.5;
- an `expected_rate` of at least 3 times `noise_rate`.

Each jurisdiction was run once.

### Reading

- **The extractor reads a flipped table symbol.** Douglas shows it: 13 of 13 against 2.6% noise, on rows the Z0.4 text could not target. The extractor followed the table text even though the raw text still showed the original symbols.
- **On Loudoun the stage's measure is diluted.** Repeated row labels across tables bring in cells whose column was not flipped. A third of the cells whose column was flipped held their conditional status.
- **The Z0.5 exit "mutation test shows the pipeline changes with the text" is met on one of the two jurisdictions** under the pre-set criterion.
- Strengthening it needs two more things:
  - column-level flip attribution in the stage;
  - flipping a row's raw and table copies together;
  - repeated runs per jurisdiction, so that a flip's effect is measured against run-to-run variation rather than a single pair. The Z0.4-base Lake runs showed that variation.

## Caveats for Z0.6

- **Permitted cells are scarce in every output:** 33 passed in codex, 27 in grok and 40 in the extractor. That is below Z0.6's minimum of 50 per status.
- **Pittsylvania codex and grok disagree heavily.** They agree on 17 of the 26 cells that both passed. Codex rejected 66 of its 98 cells, mostly unlisted cells that fail the absence gate. Z0.6 should look at why before it treats their agreement as a reference.
- **Douglas extractor and grok** agree on only 84 of 149 cells.
- **Lake** is an unadopted draft (as in Z0.4).
- **Loudoun's** datacenter cells mix the 2023 table with the 2025 amendment.
- **Grok's Loudoun output** has no cell for 25 of the 399 (district, use class) pairs that codex covers.
