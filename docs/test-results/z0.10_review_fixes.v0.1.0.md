### SUPERSEDED gate

`gates.superseded_errors(cell, amendments, sources, jurisdiction, text)` applies to each amendment record with `kind: adopted_text_amendment` and `status: adopted` in the cell's jurisdiction.

1. **When it touches a cell.** The record touches the cell when its `use_classes` contains the cell's class or `"*"`, and its `districts` contains the cell's district or `"*"`.
2. **Dates compared.** The record's effective date is `dates.effective`, else `dates.adopted`. The cited source's date is its `source_date`, else the jurisdiction's `code_version`. If either date is unknown, the gate fails closed.
3. **When it fails.** A touched cell fails when the effective date is after the cited source's date, unless it is exempt:
   - (a) the cell cites the amendment's own source, as Loudoun IP and GI cite `us-51107-dc-phase1`; or
   - (b) the record's `incorporated_in` gives a quote that is found in the cited source, which proves the cited text already contains the amendment.
4. **Where it runs, and what the reason says.** `grade` withholds the cell with `gate: superseded`, and the release gate applies the same check. The reason names the amendment id and its reference. The release gate also checks every amendment quote, and every `incorporated_in` quote, against its source.

**Records:**

- **`us-51107-zoam-2024-0001` (ZOAM-2024-0001).** Adopted 2025-03-18, class `datacenter`, districts IP, GI and MRHI. The quote comes from the already registered county page `us-51107-dc-phase1` on www.loudoun.gov and was verified against the cached text. Nothing new was fetched. The county's grandfathering of applications accepted before 2025-02-12 is not modelled.

## 6. Deferred, with reasons

1. **Subdistrict columns** (Loudoun TC, TRC and PDRV, 14 published cells before). This needs a multi-column rule that requires every subdistrict column to agree, plus a decision on how to cite it. The cells stay withheld until then.
2. **GIS subarea codes** (Loudoun TR1LF, TR1UBF, TR3LBR, TR3LF and TR3UBF, 24 published cells before). The binding to TR-1 and TR-3 is plausible, but no cached primary text supports it. A quoted source for the GIS subarea codes would restore them through `column_aliases`.
3. **Continuation pages without a repeated header** (us-12069, 18 cells). The review's rule is to bind within a page unless the header repeats. A detected continuation rule, such as the same column count with the table continuing from the previous page, is a maintainer decision and was not added.
4. **Prohibited-by-default cells.** Finding 6 names unlisted and prohibited_by_default cells. The cap covers `unlisted` only, because no published cell rests on the unlisted-use clause: none of the six jurisdictions has a published `prohibited` prose cell that quotes its `unlisted_rule_quote`. Such a cell would also be a legend-style shortcut, like finding 1, and should be rejected rather than capped.
5. **Finding 6 alternative.** Failing closed on near-miss OCR tokens was not built. The grade-C cap is the chosen rule; it limits the grade and does not prove absence.
6. **ZOAM-2024-0001 grandfathering** (applications accepted before 2025-02-12) is not modelled in the amendment record.

## 7. CI fix carried from #9
