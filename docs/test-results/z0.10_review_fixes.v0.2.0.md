### What moved, and why

Every change was checked against the ledgers before and after.

- **us-13097, 10 grok labels newly rejected by the prose-grid rule** (finding 1b). Grok cited prose for HI, LI, LI-C, LI-R and LI-R-C × `warehouse_distribution` and `heavy_industrial_hazmat`. For warehouses the quote was the unlisted-use sentence, "Any principal use not shown on the following Table 2.2 as allowed ... ", while Table 2.2 lists the use. For hazmat it was "(6) Chemical manufacturing, except pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing.", while Table 2.2 has chemical manufacturing rows under those columns. Six of these keys are published, from the extractor's table cells, and none changed status:
  - 4 were `disputed` and become `single`; they were already C;
  - 2 (LI and LI-R `heavy_industrial_hazmat`, `prohibited`) were `agree` at B and become `single` at C.
  - The rule matches class terms in row labels broadly: the warehouse match is "Warehouse clubs, supercenters, and other general merchandise retailers". It fails closed, since only prose evidence is affected, but a narrower match is a maintainer decision (section 6).
- **Absence explanations whose section sits in another source** (finding d). These counts of unexplained occurrences rose in already-rejected labels; none was published. Two extractor cells, us-51061 M-G and M-R `warehouse_distribution`, became extraction rejections: the "Mini-storage warehouses" explanation quotes Article 15 but names section 4-1002, which occurs only in Article 4. Both keys were already withheld; in `withheld.json` they move from the review gate to the extraction gate, so Fauquier's split goes from 125 extraction and 9 review to 127 and 7. A Loudoun example: grok's `canopy` explanations quote Chapter 12 but cite section 3.01, which occurs only in Chapter 3.
- **us-5183136 grok labels** that were already rejected also fail the unlisted-rule check (finding 3). They are `prohibited` labels quoting "uses not specifically listed are prohibited".
- **Nothing else changed.** The us-13097, us-51107 and us-5183136 `cells.withheld.json` files differ only in run ids.

## 6. Deferred, with reasons

None of the 10 findings is deferred. Round 1's deferrals (subdistrict columns, GIS subarea codes, continuation pages, near-miss OCR tokens, ZOAM-2024-0001 grandfathering) stand. Round 1's item 4 (prohibited-by-default prose) is now closed by finding 3. One new item:

- **Prose-grid row matching.** Finding 1b matches any taxonomy term of the class in a row label, so retail rows such as "Warehouse clubs, supercenters" count as placing `warehouse_distribution`. This can only reject prose evidence, never publish a cell. Narrowing it, for example with the taxonomy's exclusions, is a maintainer decision.
